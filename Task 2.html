Crocodile Enters Koppadi Village, Bites Resident

An 8-9 foot crocodile was found hiding under the steps of a house in Cuddalore district

By Staff Reporter | Published: 22 November 2026

An 8- to 9-foot crocodile entered Koppadi village near Kattumannarkoil in Cuddalore district early on Sunday and bit a resident named Rajendran.

The crocodile came from a nearby canal around 3:00 AM and hid under the steps of his house. When Rajendran stepped out after hearing dogs bark, it attacked him.

Neighbours helped free him and took him to Chidambaram Government Hospital. Forest Department staff secured the crocodile and it was released into Vakkamarai lake.
